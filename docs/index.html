A product that improves itself

Accuracy isn't a marketing claim — it's measured, and it compounds. The accuracy flywheel turns live operation into a continuously better model.

1

Snapshot

The edge pushes an annotated frame + its predicted count (opt-in, privacy-first — no continuous video).

2

Label

A human confirms the true count in the dashboard. Every judgement becomes a durable training sample.

3

Measure

Per-camera, per-vertical accuracy (MAE, % error) is computed from the samples — an honest, auditable number.

4

Retrain & promote

Once enough labels accrue, a count-gated retrain produces a better model and promotes it per site.
