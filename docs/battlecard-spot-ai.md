# Battlecard: CamAI vs Spot AI

_Rep-facing. One page. As of 2026-10-09. Deep dive: [competitor-spot-ai.md](competitor-spot-ai.md)._

## Who they are (10-sec)
Spot AI = well-funded (~$100M) camera-agnostic **video AI + VMS**. Smart recorder
(their **IVR** appliance, on-board GPU) that keeps full-res video on-site, adds
**natural-language video search** and **"AI agents"** for security/safety/ops.
Enterprise, sales-led, multi-year, **~$99/camera/month + hardware**.

## Qualify fast

| ✅ We win when the buyer… | ⚠️ Walk away / partner when they… |
|---|---|
| Wants **analytics** (foot traffic, queue, parking, traffic, staffing), not just security | Primarily need a **VMS / video storage + search** |
| Is **price-sensitive / SMB / multi-site**, wants self-serve | Have a mandate for a full enterprise security platform + 24/7 monitoring |
| Can't let **video leave the building** (privacy/compliance) | Want to **ask the camera questions in plain English** as the #1 feature |
| Needs **live-in-minutes**, hates procurement | Are already deep in a Verkada/Genetec stack and happy |

## 30-second pitch
> "Spot turns your cameras into a searchable security system — and charges like one
> (~$99/cam/mo, hardware, 5-year deals). CamAI is **metered multi-vertical analytics**:
> retail foot traffic, queues, parking, traffic, safety — on your existing cameras,
> **with no video ever leaving your site**, live in minutes, at a **fraction of the
> cost**. If you want analytics across the business, not just a smarter NVR, that's us."

## Why we win (lead with these)
1. **Multi-vertical breadth** — retail/queue/parking/traffic/staffing are *not* Spot's product. One pipeline, many analytics.
2. **Privacy** — only counts/events leave site; **no video, no imagery**. Spot still records/retains full-res video.
3. **Price + self-serve** — **~3–6× lower run-rate**; ONVIF auto-discovery + guided calibration vs Spot's "in-person, cabling" setup.
4. **Published accuracy** — label→retrain flywheel gives an auditable ±% number, not a marketing claim.
5. **Granular loitering/intrusion** — reviews flag Spot as weak here; we have dedicated verticals.

## Landmines to plant (discovery questions)
- "Does your video / imagery ever leave the building? What does compliance say?" → (privacy)
- "Can it count foot traffic, queue wait, parking occupancy, or traffic flow — or just security events?" → (breadth)
- "What's the all-in 5-year cost for 50 cameras, including hardware and the contract?" → (price)
- "How long from signed to live — and does it need an on-site installer and cabling?" → (onboarding)
- "What's the *measured* accuracy on your cameras, and how do you prove it?" → (flywheel)

## Objection handling

| They say… | You say… |
|---|---|
| "Spot has natural-language video search / AI agents." | "True — it's also a VMS. If you need to *search video*, Spot's great. If you need **business analytics with no video leaving site at a fraction of the cost**, that's a different product — us. We can integrate with your VMS." |
| "Spot is proven / better funded." | "They are — on security VMS. We're not rebuilding a VMS; we're the **multi-vertical analytics layer** they don't focus on, priced for self-serve." |
| "We already have cameras / don't want new hardware." | "Same — we're **software on your existing cameras**. No IVR appliance, no rip-and-replace." |
| "Isn't cheaper = less capable?" | "Cheaper because we send **counts, not video** — near-zero cloud cost. You pay for analytics, not storage you may not need." |

## Pricing snapshot
- **Spot:** ~**$99/cam/mo** + IVR hardware. 50 cams / 5 sites / 5 yr ≈ **$317k–$327k** all-in.
- **CamAI:** **$15–40/cam/mo**, metered, self-serve. Same 50 cams ≈ **$9k–$24k/yr**, no 5-yr lock-in.

## Proof points
- 16+ verticals on one edge pipeline (retail, queue, capacity, crowd, staffing, parking, traffic, drive-thru, loitering, intrusion, tailgating, wrong-way, fall, + safety/fire/thermal/proximity/weapon/abandoned).
- Verified live on **public cameras** (real YOLO detections; 2,300+ DOT cams catalogued).
- Edge-first contract: outbound-only, no raw video by default.

## Do / Don't
- **Do:** lead with analytics breadth + privacy + price + speed-to-live.
- **Do:** concede video search/retention; offer to **integrate**, not replace.
- **Don't:** get drawn into a "better AI agents / better search" bake-off — that's their turf.
- **Don't:** over-promise weapon/fire/thermal as live today — those need trained models/sensors.
